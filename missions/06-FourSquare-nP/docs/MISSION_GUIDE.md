# Mission 06: FourSquare
FourSquare models a playground-style group game with players, turns, rounds, and scoring.

## Mission Goal
Build a digital group game that tracks positions, turns, and round outcomes.

## Mode
`nP`: many players.

## Play It
Join a round and see how players, positions, and turns are represented.

## Change It
Change court labels, round messages, scoring rules, or player colors.

## Show It
Run the changed version and explain the new rule.

## Level It Up
Add custom rules, team rotations, tournament mode, or a referee/teacher view.
